# Generic image library used by CTN v1.3

These images are used as generic category fallbacks when an article has no CTN-uploaded cover. They are not Hiru News or Ada Derana images. At the time of selection, their Wikimedia Commons pages state CC0 / public-domain dedication.

- Local — Colombo city skyline: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Colombo_city_skyline.png
- World — World Map Land: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:World_Map_Land.png
- Business — Meeting public: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meeting_public.jpg
- Markets / Reports — Stock market image public domain: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stock_market_Image_public_domain.jpg
- Sports — Wayanad Cricket Stadium: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wayanad_Cricket_Stadium.png
- Entertainment — Concert crowd (Unsplash): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Concert_crowd_(Unsplash).jpg
- Technology — Artificial-Intelligence: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Artificial-Intelligence.jpg
- Train-specific fallback — Sri LAnks Old Train: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sri_LAnks_Old_Train.jpg

For a production newsroom, replacing these generic images with CTN-owned/commissioned/approved licensed photography is preferable.
